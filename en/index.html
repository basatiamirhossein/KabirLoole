Kabirloole at a glance

Rooted in Ilam, flowing through Iran's farms

Eng. Enayat Basati started out in 1995 with a workshop making nylon bags, and moved into polyethylene pipe manufacturing in 2001. Today Kabirloole, the first polyethylene pipe producer in Ilam Province, supports irrigation and water supply projects across western Iran with an experienced team and more than 5,000 tonnes of annual capacity.

Why Kabirloole

Quality that starts in the lab

  • Accredited laboratory

    A qualified lab and partner of Iran's national standards organization, with a full set of test equipment.

  • National standard mark

    Licensed to use the Iranian standard mark for polyethylene pipe production.

  • ISO certified

    Certified to ISO 9001, ISO 10006 and ISO/IEC 17025.

  • Reliable raw material supply

    5,060 tonnes of raw material capacity a year and 110 tonnes of weekly buying power on the commodity exchange.

  • Three production lines

    German, Chinese and Iranian extruders in two halls covering 1,600 m².

  • First in Ilam

    The first polyethylene pipe producer in Ilam Province, with an experienced crew and a capable engineering team.

Products
