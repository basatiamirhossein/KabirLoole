HDPE pipe

What is HDPE?

High-density polyethylene (HDPE) is a tough, light and flexible polymer. Pipe made from it resists corrosion, soil chemicals and impact, making it a dependable alternative to metal and concrete pipe for carrying water.

  • Production range: 16 – 630 mm
  • PE100 · PE80
  • Iranian national standard mark

Wall thickness, weight and pressure class

SDR is the ratio of a pipe's outside diameter to its wall thickness: the lower the SDR, the thicker the wall and the higher the working pressure. All values here come from Kabirloole's own factory spec sheet, per Iranian national standard INSO 14427-2.

Products

Applications

  • Drip & sprinkler irrigation

    Main and lateral pipe for pressurized irrigation systems.

  • Agricultural water transmission

    Lines from wells, springs and ponds to fields and orchards.

  • Urban & rural water supply

    Distribution networks and water utility projects.

  • Civil works

    Municipal projects and civil engineering contractors.

Products

Why polyethylene pipe

  • Rust-free

    Resists corrosion and soil chemicals.

  • Flexible

    Withstands impact, vibration and ground settlement.

  • Light & economical

    Quick and easy to handle, transport and install.

  • UV resistant

    Contains carbon black at the standard level.

  • Leak-free joints

    Heat-fusion weldable and compatible with compression fittings.

  • Long service life

    Designed for 50 years of service under standard conditions.

Products

Packing & supply

  • Coils

    Smaller diameters are coiled for easy transport and installation in the field.

  • Straight lengths

    Larger diameters are supplied in straight lengths for transmission lines and civil works.
