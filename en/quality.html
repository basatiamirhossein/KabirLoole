Quality & Lab

Every pipe is tested before it reaches the field

Kabirloole runs an accredited laboratory, a partner of Iran's national standards organization, fully equipped to test raw material and finished pipe.

Quality & Lab

Laboratory equipment

From dimensional checks to hydrostatic pressure testing, every production batch is checked with this equipment.

  • Hydrostatic pressure tester

    Pipe resistance to internal pressure at a set temperature and time.

  • OIT analyzer

    Oxidation induction time and thermal stability of the material.

  • MFI tester

    Melt flow index of raw material and finished pipe.

  • Carbon black tester

    Carbon black content for UV resistance.

  • Trinocular microscope

    Carbon black dispersion and material uniformity.

  • Oven

    Sample conditioning and thermal tests.

  • Tensile tester

    Tensile strength and elongation at break.

  • 5-tonne dumbbell cutter

    Standard dumbbell specimens for tensile testing.

  • Density kit

    Density of polyethylene material.

  • Four-decimal balance

    High-precision sample weighing.

  • Circometer 50–300 mm

    Mean outside diameter of large pipe.

  • Circometer 15–115 mm

    Mean outside diameter of small pipe.

  • Caliper 0–200 mm

    Wall thickness and dimensions.

  • Caliper 0–300 mm

    Dimensions of large-diameter pipe.
